Keyboard dictation is back on its feet.

• The keyboard mic works again after the first session: later taps start recording right inside the keyboard instead of failing or bouncing to the app.
• New Cancel button on the keyboard while recording or transcribing.
• Clearer status when a recording can't start, instead of silently returning to the keys.
• Cloud dictation streams live through Writingmate when you're signed in; otherwise it falls back to standard upload.
• Fixes for the app getting stuck behind storage checks at launch.
