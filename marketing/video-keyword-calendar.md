## Reusable 55-Second Structure

- **0–3 seconds:** Put the exact search query on screen and state the outcome.
- **3–10 seconds:** Show the problem in a familiar text field.
- **10–38 seconds:** Demonstrate the current product without hidden cuts.
- **38–49 seconds:** Show the resulting text and one relevant control.
- **49–55 seconds:** “AI Dictation is open source. Try it at aidictation.com.”

## Claim Guardrails

- Say “supported text fields,” not “every app.”
- Say “offline recognition on supported devices,” not “fully offline everywhere.”
- If cloud cleanup is enabled, disclose that transcript text may be processed in
  the cloud even when speech recognition ran locally.
- Do not claim the best accuracy, fastest output, clinical suitability,
  accessibility certification, or regulatory compliance without dated,
  reproducible evidence.
- Competitor videos must disclose project ownership, use the same sample and
  settings, show uncut outputs, and cite facts checked on the recording date.
- Use synthetic data for medical, financial, legal, or other sensitive examples.
- Avoid price claims until every public pricing page agrees.
