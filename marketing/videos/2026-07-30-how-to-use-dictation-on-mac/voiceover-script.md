# Voice-over script

Target runtime: 45–60 seconds. Voice: `ash`, upbeat and helpful. The script deliberately avoids accuracy, speed, privacy, and competitor-performance comparisons.

## 1. Title

Here’s how to use dictation on a Mac — with Apple’s built-in option, and with AI Dictation.

## 2. Turn on built-in Dictation

For built-in Dictation, open System Settings, choose Keyboard, and turn Dictation on. Pick your language, microphone, and shortcut.

## 3. Use built-in Dictation

Place your cursor in a text field. Press the Microphone key, your shortcut, or choose Edit, Start Dictation. Speak, then press Escape.

## 4. Start AI Dictation

AI Dictation starts from a configurable global shortcut. Hold it for push-to-talk, or double-tap to start and stop a longer recording.

## 5. Choose AI Dictation options

You can choose local recognition when supported or cloud transcription, then add optional cleanup, personal vocabulary, replacements, writing rules, and spoken shortcuts.

## 6. Return the text

When processing finishes, AI Dictation returns the final text to the active app, ready where you were already writing.

## 7. Call to action

Start with built-in Dictation. When you need more control, try the open-source AI Dictation at aidictation dot com slash download.
