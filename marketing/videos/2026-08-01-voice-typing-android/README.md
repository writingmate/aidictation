## Creative direction

The title and thumbnail lead with the exact search intent. The hook is “keep your keyboard,” shown beside a real current Android capture of the floating microphone. Motion is restrained: short spring entrances, slow screenshot zooms, and one genuine before/result cross-fade. Privacy and cloud-processing caveats are stated in plain language.
